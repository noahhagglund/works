hello! my name is noah hägglund.

I am a Sound composer, designer and engineer. I create sound design and music for games, animation and film.

over the past several years, i have honed my craft by working on commercial film and television projects, specializing in audio production for short format promotional films.

however, I have also successfully applied my skills to longer formats, such as dramas & documentaries.

my expertise lies in capturing & crafting high-quality audio that enhances the overall production value of these projects.

services

I specialize in creating high-quality audio for various media projects. i offer a range of services that include

sound design

design and create custom sound effects that are tailored to your project's specific needs. from explosions and gunfire to footsteps and ambient soundscapes.

mixing and mastering

mix & master your project's audio to ensure that all the sounds are balanced & sound their best. whether you need a simple voiceover or a complex mix of music, dialogue & sound effects.

audio restoration

restore & clean up existing audio to improve its quality. this could be anything from removing unwanted noise, hiss or hum to restoring the dynamics & clarity of the sound.

I'm constantly striving to further develop my workflow & my own sound. this, of course, without ever sacrificing the quality of my craft. I'm always keen on going the extra mile for that premium end product. as a side fling, i indulge in crafting unique sounds using Ableton. whether it's gritty electronic dance music, experimental beats or rich soundscapes, my primary goal is to evoke emotions and produce unique, captivating music that engages the listener. my focus is on creating sounds that are not only interesting but also reflective of my personal style and creative expression.
