Hello! My name is Noah Hägglund.

I am a sound composer, designer and engineer. I create sound design and music for games, animation and film.

Over the past several years, I have honed my craft by working on commercial film and television projects, specializing in audio production for short format promotional films.

However, I have also successfully applied my skills to longer formats, such as dramas & documentaries.

My expertise lies in capturing & crafting high-quality audio that enhances the overall production value of these projects.

Services

I specialize in creating high-quality audio for various media projects. I can help with a range of services that include:

Sound Design

Design and create custom sound effects tailored to your project's specific needs. From detailed environments and movement to dramatic effects and atmospheric soundscapes.

Mixing & Mastering

Mix and master your project's audio so dialogue, music and sound effects work together clearly and effectively.

Audio Restoration

Clean up and restore existing recordings by removing unwanted noise, hiss, hum and other distractions while preserving the character of the original recording.

I'm constantly striving to further develop my workflow & my own sound. This, of course, without ever sacrificing the quality of my craft. I'm always keen on going the extra mile for that premium end product.

As a side fling, i indulge in crafting unique sounds using Ableton. Whether it's gritty electronic dance music, experimental beats or rich soundscapes, my primary goal is to evoke emotions and produce unique, captivating music that engages the listener.
